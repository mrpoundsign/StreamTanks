GAME LOOP BASICS

How to Play in 3 Simple Steps

You don't need to download or install any external software. As long as you're in the streamer's Twitch chat, you can jump into the battle immediately!

1

Join the Battlefield

When the stream overlay shows WAITING FOR PLAYERS, type %join in Twitch chat. You can also specify any channel emote to crown your tank!

Twitch Chat 
# Join with default icon:
%join

# Join with a custom channel emote:
%join Kappa

Your tank will parachute onto the battlefield crowned with your chosen emote and glowing neon treads.

2

Aim & Position (Input Phase)

Once the match begins, a countdown timer starts (default: 20s). This is your window to dial in your angle and power, or move to high ground:

  • Aim & Fire: Type %fire <angle> <power> (e.g. %fire 45 60). A visual aiming protractor appears above your tank on stream showing your aim!
  • Move Left or Right: Type %left or %right to maneuver across hills and get out of dangerous craters.
  • Emergency Shield: Type %shield to activate your one-time protective energy barrier.
Twitch Chat 
%fire 55 70
%left
3

Simultaneous Barrage & Victory!

When the timer hits zero, the ACTION PHASE begins! All artillery shells fire simultaneously into the sky:

  • Shells arc with real ballistic gravity and explode on impact, carving spherical craters into the neon hills.
  • Tanks hit by explosions or falling into deep crater chasms are eliminated.
  • The last surviving commander wins +5 bonus victory points and is celebrated on the persistent stream leaderboard with an epic emote shower!
BALLISTICS & DEFORMATION

Aiming Protractors & Physics Engine

Mastering angles and projectile physics is the key to dominating the stream leaderboard.

📐

The 0° to 180° Protractor

Angles are measured from right to left:
• 0°: Flat shot aimed straight to the right.
• 45°: Classic 45-degree arcing trajectory to the right.
• 90°: Straight up into the sky (watch out for wind & falling shells!).
• 135°: Classic 45-degree arcing trajectory to the left.
• 180°: Flat shot aimed straight to the left.

🎚️

Shot Power (1% to 100%)

Power controls muzzle velocity and trajectory reach:
• Low Power (10–30%): Short-range lobbing, ideal for nearby tanks tucked in adjacent craters.
• Mid Power (40–70%): Standard cross-map artillery strikes.
• High Power (80–100%): Extreme long-distance bombardments and high-altitude sky drops.

💥

Destructible Craters & Gravity

Missed shells aren't wasted—they permanently carve out large craters in the neon ground. Tanks caught on steep crater edges slide toward the bottom, reshaping the entire battlefield round after round!

🧱

Bouncy Screen Walls

If the streamer enables %bouncywalls, the left and right screen borders act as kinetic bounce pads. Shells reflect with a +10% velocity boost, allowing incredible bank shots off the screen edge!

🧗

Terrain Climbing & Cliffs

By default, tanks cannot scale steep crater cliffs. Use %left and %right to climb gentle slopes. If the streamer enables %terrainclimb on, tanks can scale steep crater walls.

🎯

Memory Aiming

If you like your current aim, typing just %fire without numbers will repeat your previous angle and power. Great for rapid adjustments after seeing where your last shot landed!

COMMANDER TACTICS

Player FAQ & Winning Tips

Do I need to install anything to play?

Never! StreamTanks is 100% chat-driven. All you need is your regular Twitch account and chat box. When the StreamTanks Controller extension for Twitch is active, you can also aim directly on the video player.

How does the 5% bounty work?

Whenever you eliminate an opponent, you steal 5% of their total leaderboard score (rounded down: score / 20) in addition to 1 base point. Eliminating the #1 leaderboard leader can vault you straight into the top ranks!

What angle should I use to shoot far?

In basic physics, 45° (for right-side targets) and 135° (for left-side targets) provide maximum horizontal range for a given power. If high hills or crater walls block your direct line of sight, try 60°–75° high-angle lobs!

Does my score reset when I leave the stream?

No. All leaderboard stats and win records are saved in the streamer's persistent database. Whenever you return to the stream, your score, wins, and custom emote crown are right where you left them.
