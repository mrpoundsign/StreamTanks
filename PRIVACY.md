# StreamTanks Privacy Policy

The StreamTanks Twitch Extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data, tracking cookies, or personally identifiable information (PII). 

When you interact with the extension, your Twitch User ID and display name are temporarily used in-memory to route your gameplay commands (such as aiming and firing) to the broadcaster's game client. This data is processed in real-time and is never saved to a database, sold, or shared with third parties.

StreamTanks is 100% open-source. You can verify this behavior by reviewing the source code at: https://github.com/mrpoundsign/StreamTanks
